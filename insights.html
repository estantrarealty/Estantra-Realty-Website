RENTAL GUIDE

Documents to organise for a Leave & License Agreement

Starting with complete, consistent details helps reduce drafting delays. Before you begin, make one folder for the property, the owner, and the tenant.

Property & agreement details

  • Complete property address and society/building details
  • Monthly rent, refundable deposit, term, lock-in period, and payment date
  • Any furnishings, parking, maintenance, or special conditions to be recorded

Party details

  • Names and contact details exactly as used in the parties’ documents
  • Identity and address documents requested through the authorised process
  • Ownership or authority documents, where applicable

Checklist only: the right documents and registration steps can vary by property, parties, and the applicable official workflow. Ask for a tailored checklist before sharing documents.

LANDLORD CHECKLIST

Tenant verification: prepare before move-in

Tenant verification is easier when the landlord and tenant agree on the process before move-in. Keep your records organised and seek assistance with the relevant official or society requirements.

  1. Confirm the tenant’s complete contact details and proposed move-in date.
  2. Check the society’s move-in rules, notice format, and any forms required.
  3. Prepare a clear inventory or condition record for furnished properties.
  4. Keep a copy of the completed agreement and delivery/possession notes.
  5. Use an authorised or appropriate process for police-verification assistance where required.

Do not treat informal online messages as a substitute for the applicable verification or society process.

PROPERTY SUPPORT

Rental renewals and society paperwork: plan early

Set a reminder 30–45 days before an agreement ends. This leaves time to confirm revised rent, deposit, new term, and any changes in occupants or property terms.

For renewals

  • Review the current agreement rather than copying it blindly.
  • Record changes in rent, deposit, term, notice period, or occupancy.
  • Confirm the required registration-assistance steps in advance.

For society documents

  • Confirm the society’s current document checklist.
  • Keep receipts, NOCs, and correspondence in one folder.
  • Use a document handover list whenever originals are involved.

BUSINESS DOCUMENTATION

Commercial lease: the questions to settle before drafting

Commercial leases need more planning than a standard residential rental arrangement. Decide the business points first, then involve an advocate where legal drafting or review is required.

  • Permitted business use and licences required for that activity
  • Lease term, lock-in, escalation, deposit, and renewal rights
  • Maintenance, repair, signage, fit-out, insurance, and utility responsibilities
  • Exit, handover, and dispute-resolution provisions

This is general information, not legal advice. Commercial terms should be reviewed for the specific property and business.
