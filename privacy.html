Privacy Notice

This notice explains how the current Estantra Realty website handles information you choose to share. Review and update it before adding an account system, document-upload portal, payment gateway, CRM, analytics platform or newsletter provider.

Information you choose to provide

When you use the callback or checklist form, the website opens WhatsApp with the details you entered. When you use the newsletter form, your mail application opens with a subscription request. This static website does not independently store those form details.

How to share documents safely

Please do not send Aadhaar, PAN, bank details, ownership documents or other sensitive documents through WhatsApp unless Estantra Realty gives you a secure authorised method and explains why the document is needed.

Contact and newsletter requests

Information you send to estantrarealty@gmail.com or WhatsApp may be used to reply to your request, coordinate requested services and, only where you have opted in, send the requested newsletter. You can ask to stop newsletter messages at any time.

External services

The site uses Google Fonts and links to WhatsApp and your email application. These providers may process information under their own policies when you use their services.

Contact

For privacy questions or to withdraw a newsletter request, email estantrarealty@gmail.com.
